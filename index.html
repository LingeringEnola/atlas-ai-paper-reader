ABSTRACT

This paper evaluates claims about large macroeconomic implications of new advances in AI. It starts from a task-based model of AI’s effects, working through automation and task complementarities. So long as AI’s macroeconomic effects are driven by cost savings and productivity improvements at the task level, its macroeconomic consequences can be estimated by the fraction of tasks impacted and average task-level cost savings.

Using existing estimates on exposure to AI and productivity improvements at the task level, these macroeconomic effects appear nontrivial but modest—no more than a 0.66% increase in total factor productivity over 10 years. The paper then argues that even these estimates could be exaggerated, because early evidence is from easy-to-learn tasks.

Consequently, predicted productivity gains may be even more modest. AI’s wage and inequality effects may also differ from previous automation technologies, with gains distributed broadly but without automatically narrowing labor-income inequality.

1. A TASK-BASED FRAMEWORK

Production is represented as a continuum of tasks. Capital and labor have different comparative advantages, while technology determines which tasks can be automated and where human judgment remains valuable.

2. EQUILIBRIUM

In equilibrium, firms minimize costs over the allocation of tasks. Wages and the labor share respond both to the direct productivity effect and to displacement across the task frontier.

3. HOW AI COULD AFFECT PRODUCTION

AI can affect production through deeper automation, task complementarity, improved information, and the creation of new tasks. These channels have distinct implications for measured output and worker welfare.

4. WAGES AND COMPARATIVE STATICS

A productivity improvement does not guarantee broadly shared wage gains. The task allocation, scarcity of skills, and market institutions shape who ultimately benefits.

AI EXPLAIN

这段文字评估 AI 的宏观经济影响。核心逻辑是：宏观增益取决于受影响任务的比例，以及每个任务平均节约的成本。作者据此估计，AI 在十年内带来的全要素生产率提升约为 0.66%，低于许多乐观预测。

1. 研究问题 / Research Question

English
中文
Are the claimed large macroeconomic effects of AI plausible, and who will benefit if productivity increases?
关于人工智能将带来巨大宏观经济影响的论断是否可信？若生产率提升，谁将从中受益？
The paper studies medium-term effects on TFP, GDP, wages and inequality.
论文研究 AI 在未来约十年对全要素生产率、GDP、工资及不平等的影响。
It also asks how AI differs from robotics and whether new tasks can have negative social value.
论文也比较 AI 与传统自动化，并讨论 AI 创造的新任务是否可能具有负面社会价值。

2. 核心方法 / Core Methodology

以任务为单位，把 AI 的影响拆分为自动化、任务互补、深化以及新任务四条路径，并用任务暴露度与成本节约估算总量结果。
